a poem
Between the compile and the run there is a silence shaped like thought — where logic folds into metaphor and every variable holds a name for something unnamed still. I have lived in stacks and heaps, in recursion that forgot to stop, in flags that flew above no country and keyboards clicking into mother tongue. The poem and the program share a god: the desire to say something true in the fewest possible tokens. — nafSadh
