I’m Sadh — Khan Muhammad Nafee Mostafa Sadh, if you want the full thing. I write code, words, and verses. I paint on canvas and in light. Born in the quiet town of Tangail, schooled in Dhaka, degreed in computer science at KUET, then a master’s at Stony Brook University. Now in the Bay Area, building the infrastructure for AI that helps people think.

Senior Software Engineer at Google DeepMind, working on the Gemini API and agentic capabilities — the surface through which the model meets the world.
