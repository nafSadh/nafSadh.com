I am Sadh MNM Khan, aka nafSadh@, software engineer, aspiring artist, painter, photographer, poet and storyteller.

Most of my time goes into building infrastructure on which generative AI runs. Besides that, I enjoy being able to explore and express, and I want to enable everyone to be able to do so.

Recently I've been working on a few stories, a collection of poems. When the outdoor lights are nice, I often pick up my cameras and wander around, by foot or car. I share many of my personal works on nafSadh.com and related sites.
